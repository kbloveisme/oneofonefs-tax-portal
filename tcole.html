Texas Commission on Law Enforcement — Basic Peace Officer Curriculum
📌
TCOLE Basic Peace Officer Course — 618 Hours Minimum

All persons seeking a Texas Peace Officer license must complete the Basic Peace Officer Course approved by TCOLE, pass the licensing exam, and meet all requirements under TCOLE Rule 217.11.

TCOLE Certification Levels

📄 Continuing Education Requirements
Mandatory Annual Training
  • Firearms qualification (annual)
  • CPR/First Aid (biennial)
  • Mental Health officer training (biennial)
  • Racial profiling training (annual)
  • Human trafficking (as required by TCOLE)
40-Hour Biennial Requirement
  • All licensed peace officers must complete 40 hours of continuing education every 2 years
  • Must report training to TCOLE through their agency
  • Failure results in license suspension
